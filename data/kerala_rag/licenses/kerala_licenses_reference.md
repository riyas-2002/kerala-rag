# Kerala Business Licenses and Permits — Quick Reference

## 1. Trade License

**Governing Law**: Kerala Municipality Act, 1994; Kerala Panchayat Raj Act, 1994

**Issuing Authority**:
- Municipal Corporations (Thiruvananthapuram, Kochi, Kozhikode, Thrissur, Kollam, Kannur, Palakkad)
- Municipalities
- Gram Panchayats

**Who Needs It**: Every commercial establishment operating within a local body's jurisdiction.

**Renewal**: Annually before 31st March.

**Penalty for Non-compliance**: ₹500–₹5,000 fine; business closure notice.

## 2. FSSAI — Food Safety License

**Governing Law**: Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006

**Issuing Authority**: Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)

**Types**:
| Type | Applicability | Annual Fee |
|------|--------------|------------|
| Basic Registration | Turnover < ₹12 lakh | ₹100 |
| State License | Turnover ₹12 lakh–₹20 crore | ₹2,000–₹5,000 |
| Central License | Turnover > ₹20 crore; importers; multi-state | ₹7,500 |

**Key applicability**: Restaurants, hotels, cloud kitchens, caterers, food manufacturers, food storage, food traders, dairy units, slaughterhouses.

**Renewal**: Annually or up to 5 years at the time of application.

**Penalty for Non-compliance**: Fine up to ₹5 lakh; imprisonment up to 6 years for unsafe food; business closure.

## 3. Fire NOC — No Objection Certificate

**Governing Law**: Kerala Fire Force Act, 1962; National Building Code of India 2016

**Issuing Authority**: Kerala Fire & Rescue Services

**Applicable Buildings**:
- Educational buildings (> 15 m height OR > 500 sqm)
- Institutional buildings (hospitals, nursing homes)
- Assembly buildings (cinemas, malls, auditoriums, restaurants with > 50 occupants)
- Business buildings (offices > 15 m height)
- Industrial buildings
- Hazardous buildings (petroleum, chemicals)
- Mixed occupancy buildings

**Required Fire Safety Installations**:
- First Aid Fire Extinguishers (ABC type / CO₂)
- Automatic sprinkler system (high-rise buildings)
- Fire alarm and detection system
- Hose reels and hydrant system
- Emergency exit signs (luminescent)
- Emergency lighting (minimum 1-hour backup)
- Fire escape staircases (for multi-storey buildings)

**Fees**: Based on floor plate area:
- Up to 500 sqm: ₹1,000
- 501–1,000 sqm: ₹2,500
- 1,001–5,000 sqm: ₹5,000
- Above 5,000 sqm: ₹10,000 + ₹1 per sqm above 5,000

## 4. Factory License

**Governing Law**: Factories Act, 1948; Kerala Factories Rules, 1957

**Issuing Authority**: Directorate of Factories and Boilers (DFB), Kerala

**Applicability**:
- Premises using power with 10+ workers, OR
- Premises without power with 20+ workers

**License Fee** (approximate):
- Based on HP of installed machinery + number of workers
- Typically ₹5,000 – ₹1,00,000+ per annum

**Renewal**: Annual — before 31st December.

**Key Compliance Requirements**:
- Adequate lighting, ventilation, cleanliness
- Drinking water facilities
- First aid facilities (1 box per 150 workers)
- Safety officer (factories with 1,000+ workers)
- Canteen (factories with 250+ workers)
- Creche (factories with 30+ women workers)
- Maximum working hours: 48/week, 9/day
- Overtime rate: Double the ordinary rate

## 5. Pollution Control Board — Consent to Establish (CTE) and Consent to Operate (CTO)

**Governing Law**: Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974; Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981; Environment Protection Act, 1986

**Issuing Authority**: Kerala State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB)

**Industry Categories**:
| Category | Description | Examples |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| Red | Highly polluting | Cement, chemicals, sugar, distillery, tannery |
| Orange | Moderately polluting | Pharmaceuticals, dyes, food processing, rubber |
| Green | Low polluting | Handloom, small engineering, small bakeries |
| White | Non-polluting | IT/ITES, software, paper trading, tailoring |

**Fee**: Based on industry category and investment:
- Green: ₹2,000 – ₹25,000
- Orange: ₹5,000 – ₹1,00,000
- Red: ₹25,000 – ₹5,00,000 (depends on investment)

**Validity**:
- CTE: Valid till completion of project (max 5 years, renewable)
- CTO: 5 years (renewable)

## 6. Shops and Commercial Establishments Registration

**Governing Law**: Kerala Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, 1960

**Issuing Authority**: Labour Department, Kerala

**Applicability**: All shops, commercial establishments, hotels, restaurants, theatres, and other establishments.

**Documents Required**:
- Application form
- Identity proof
- Address proof of establishment
- Proof of number of employees

**Fee**: Based on number of employees (approximately ₹200 – ₹2,000).

**Renewal**: Annual.

## 7. Drug License

**Governing Law**: Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940; Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945

**Issuing Authority**: Kerala State Drugs Control Department

**Types**:
| Form | For |
|------|-----|
| Form 20 | Retail sale of drugs other than Schedule X |
| Form 21 | Retail sale including Schedule X drugs |
| Form 20B | Wholesale license |
| Form 21B | Wholesale including Schedule H/X |
| Form 26D | Ayurvedic drugs — retail |
| Form 27D | Ayurvedic drugs — manufacture |

**Key Requirements**:
- Qualified pharmacist (B.Pharm / D.Pharm) as in-charge
- Proper storage conditions (temperature, humidity)
- Cold storage for Schedule X drugs (where required)
- Premises must meet DCA standards

## 8. Liquor License (BEVCO)

**Governing Law**: Kerala Abkari Act, 1077 ME; Kerala Foreign Liquor Rules

**Issuing Authority**: Kerala State Beverages Corporation (BEVCO); Excise Department, Kerala

**License Types**:
| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| FL-3 | Bar attached to hotel (3-star and above) |
| FL-9 | Supply to clubs and messes |
| Hotel Room Service | Minibar for hotel guests |

**Note**: Kerala follows restricted liquor policy. Standalone bar licenses are generally not issued. Liquor service is only permitted in hotels with star category rating by Kerala Tourism or club establishments.

## 9. Building Permit

**Governing Law**: Kerala Municipality Building Rules, 2019; Kerala Panchayat Building Rules, 2011

**Issuing Authority**: Respective Local Self-Government Body (Corporation / Municipality / Panchayat)

**Applicability**: All new constructions, additions, alterations above 3 metres height or 9 sqm area.

**Procedure**:
1. Apply online via respective LSG portal
2. Submit building plans (AutoCAD / PDF) prepared by licensed Architect / Engineer
3. Pay scrutiny fee
4. Technical scrutiny by LSG
5. Site inspection
6. Building Permit issued
7. Commence construction
8. Obtain Occupancy Certificate after completion

---

## 10. Excise License for Petroleum / LPG Dealers

**Governing Law**: Petroleum Act, 1934; Petroleum Rules, 2002; PESO (Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation)

**Issuing Authority**: PESO — Nagpur (central authority); Kerala Petroleum Dealers Association (advisory)

**Applicable to**: Petrol pumps, LPG filling stations, kerosene dealers, petroleum storage depots.

**Required Approvals**:
- PESO license for storage and handling
- NOC from District Collector
- Fire NOC from Fire & Rescue Services
- PCB NOC
- Trade License from Local Body
- Explosive License (if applicable — for storage above 25,000 litres)
