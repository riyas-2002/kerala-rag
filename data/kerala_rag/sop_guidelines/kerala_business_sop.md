# Standard Operating Procedures — Kerala Business Registration

## SOP-001: Restaurant / Food Establishment Setup

### Overview
This SOP outlines the step-by-step compliance procedure for establishing a restaurant, café, or food service establishment in Kerala.

### Pre-requisites
- Business entity formation (Proprietorship / Partnership / LLP / Pvt Ltd)
- Premises identified and lease/ownership documents ready
- PAN Card of business / proprietor

---

### Step 1: Business Entity Registration
- **Proprietorship**: No formal registration required. Obtain PAN, open current account.
- **Partnership Firm**: Execute Partnership Deed on stamp paper. Register with Registrar of Firms (optional but recommended).
- **LLP / Pvt Ltd**: Register with Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) at: https://www.mca.gov.in
- **Timeline**: 1–7 days (proprietorship instant; Pvt Ltd 7–10 days)

---

### Step 2: GST Registration
- **Mandatory if**: Turnover exceeds ₹20 lakh/year (₹10 lakh for North-East / special category states)
- **Apply at**: https://www.gst.gov.in
- **Documents**: PAN, Aadhaar, business address proof, bank account details, photographs
- **Timeline**: 3–7 working days
- **Fee**: Free

---

### Step 3: Trade License
- **Issuing Authority**: Local Municipal Corporation / Municipality / Gram Panchayat
- **Apply at**: Respective LSG body's citizen service portal or office
- **Documents required**:
  - Application form
  - Proof of premises (rental agreement / ownership deed)
  - Identity proof of proprietor/director
  - Fire NOC (see Step 5)
  - Building plan / layout sketch
- **Fee**: ₹500 – ₹10,000 (based on area and category)
- **Timeline**: 15–30 working days after document submission
- **Validity**: Annual (must renew before March 31 each year)

---

### Step 4: FSSAI License
- **Issuing Authority**: Food Safety and Standards Authority of India
- **Apply at**: https://foscos.fssai.gov.in
- **License Type**:
  - Registration (Basic): turnover < ₹12 lakh
  - State License: turnover ₹12 lakh – ₹20 crore
  - Central License: turnover > ₹20 crore
- **Documents required**:
  - Form B (application)
  - Photo ID and address proof
  - Layout plan of food handling area
  - List of food products
  - Water quality test report (for manufacturing)
  - Trade License copy
- **Fee**: State License — ₹2,000–₹5,000/year
- **Timeline**: 30–60 days
- **Validity**: 1–5 years (renewable)

---

### Step 5: Fire NOC
- **Issuing Authority**: Kerala Fire & Rescue Services
- **Applicable to**: All restaurants, commercial kitchens, establishments with public footfall
- **Apply at**: Nearest Fire Station or District Fire Office
- **Documents required**:
  - Building plan with fire safety layout
  - List of fire safety equipment installed
  - Electrical installation certificate
- **Required fire safety equipment**:
  - Portable fire extinguishers (CO₂ / dry powder)
  - Smoke detectors / heat detectors
  - Emergency exit signage
  - Emergency lighting
  - Fire alarm system (for large establishments)
- **Fee**: Based on floor area (approx. ₹1,000 – ₹10,000)
- **Timeline**: 15–30 days after inspection
- **Validity**: 1–3 years (renewable)

---

### Step 6: Shops & Establishments Registration
- **Issuing Authority**: Labour Department, Kerala
- **Purpose**: Regulates working conditions, leave, working hours for commercial establishments
- **Apply at**: Labour Department portal or nearest Labour Office
- **Fee**: Based on number of employees
- **Timeline**: 7–15 days

---

### Step 7: Professional Tax Registration
- **Issuing Authority**: Commercial Taxes Department, Kerala
- **Applicable to**: All businesses and self-employed professionals
- **Rate**: ₹1,200–₹2,400/year depending on income slab
- **Apply at**: https://www.keralataxes.gov.in

---

### Step 8: ESI & PF Registration (if employing 10+ workers)
- **ESI (ESIC)**: Mandatory for factories/establishments with 10+ employees earning ≤ ₹21,000/month
  - Apply at: https://www.esic.nic.in
- **PF (EPFO)**: Mandatory for establishments with 20+ employees
  - Apply at: https://www.epfindia.gov.in

## SOP-002: Factory / Manufacturing Unit Setup

### Step 1: Site Approval
- Identify land zone: Industrial / Commercial (per Kerala Land Use Rules)
- Obtain land conversion certificate if agricultural land

### Step 2: PCB Consent to Establish (CTE)
- **Issuing Authority**: Kerala State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB)
- **Apply at**: https://www.keralapcb.nic.in
- **Required before**: Starting construction / installation of plant
- **Documents**:
  - Industry / project report
  - Site plan and layout drawing
  - EIA Report (for Red category industries)
  - Details of raw materials, products, emissions, effluents, waste
  - Water supply source and consumption details
  - Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) design
- **Fee**: Based on investment amount (approximately ₹5,000 – ₹2,00,000)
- **Timeline**: 60–120 days for Red category; 30–60 days for Orange/Green

### Step 3: Building Permit
- Apply to Local Self-Government body for building plan approval
- Kerala Municipality Building Rules, 2019 governs this

### Step 4: Factory Registration & Plan Approval
- **Issuing Authority**: Directorate of Factories and Boilers, Kerala
- **Address**: Vikas Bhavan, Thiruvananthapuram — 695033
- **Submit**: Site plan, factory layout, machinery details, process flow
- **Purpose**: Pre-approval before construction begins

### Step 5: Construction

### Step 6: PCB Consent to Operate (CTO)
- Apply after construction completion and before starting production
- Physical inspection by PCB officials
- ETP must be operational

### Step 7: Factory License
- **Apply to**: Directorate of Factories and Boilers
- **Documents**:
  - Completed factory plan (as-built drawings)
  - Electricity connection details
  - List of machinery with horse power
  - Names of workers / employees list
  - CTO from PCB
  - Structural stability certificate
- **Validity**: Annual (renew before December 31 each year)
- **Fee**: Based on number of workers and HP of machinery

### Step 8: Labour Registrations
- Contract Labour Registration (if using contractors)
- Minimum Wages compliance
- ESI + PF registration
- Building & Other Construction Workers (BOCW) cess (for construction phase)

## SOP-003: MSME Registration (Udyam)

### Eligibility
Any micro, small, or medium enterprise engaged in manufacturing or services.

### Procedure
1. Visit: https://udyamregistration.gov.in
2. Click "For New Entrepreneurs who are not Registered yet"
3. Enter Aadhaar number of proprietor / managing partner / director
4. OTP verification via Aadhaar-linked mobile
5. Fill:
   - Name of enterprise
   - Type of organisation
   - PAN number
   - Location / address
   - Bank account details
   - NIC activity code (industry type)
   - Number of employees
   - Investment in plant & machinery
   - Turnover (previous year)
6. Submit — Certificate generated instantly
7. Download and save Udyam Registration Certificate

### Key Points
- **Free of cost** — no government fee
- **Lifetime registration** — no renewal required
- **Updates allowed** anytime online
- Benefits include: priority lending, collateral-free loans (CGTMSE), government procurement preference, electricity tariff concessions, subsidy on patents/trademarks
