# Kerala Business Compliance — Frequently Asked Questions

## Trade License

**Q: What is a Trade License?**
A Trade License is a certificate/permission issued by a Municipal Corporation or Local Self-Government Body to carry on a particular trade or business in a particular locality. It ensures that the business is carried on as per the rules and regulations of the local body and does not create any nuisance or health hazard to the general public.

**Q: Who issues Trade License in Kerala?**
In Kerala, Trade Licenses are issued by the concerned Local Self-Government (LSG) bodies:
- Municipal Corporation (Thiruvananthapuram, Kochi, Kozhikode, etc.)
- Municipalities
- Gram Panchayats

**Q: What documents are required for a Trade License?**
- Application form (available at LSG office or online)
- Proof of identity (Aadhaar, PAN)
- Proof of business address (rent agreement / ownership documents)
- No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the building owner (if rented)
- Fire NOC (for restaurants, hotels, commercial kitchens, establishments with high footfall)
- Site plan / layout plan
- Ownership or tenancy documents of premises

**Q: What is the fee for Trade License in Kerala?**
Fees vary by local body and business type. Typically ranges from ₹500 to ₹10,000 per year depending on the category and floor area of the establishment.

**Q: How long does it take to get a Trade License?**
The normal processing time is 15–30 working days after submission of complete documents.

## FSSAI License

**Q: What is FSSAI and who needs it?**
The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) regulates the food industry in India. Any Food Business Operator (FBO) in Kerala — including restaurants, cloud kitchens, caterers, food manufacturers, food importers, and traders — must obtain an FSSAI registration or license.

**Q: What type of FSSAI registration/license do I need?**
- **FSSAI Basic Registration**: Turnover up to ₹12 lakh/year; petty food business operators
- **FSSAI State License**: Turnover between ₹12 lakh and ₹20 crore/year; issued by Kerala State Food Safety
- **FSSAI Central License**: Turnover above ₹20 crore/year; importers; operators in multiple states; issued by FSSAI Central Authority

**Q: How to apply for FSSAI in Kerala?**
Apply online at: https://foscos.fssai.gov.in
Select "State License" for Kerala-based businesses (unless Central license criteria are met).

**Q: Documents required for FSSAI State License:**
- Photo ID proof
- Address proof of business premises
- List of food products to be manufactured/handled
- Blueprint / layout plan of the processing unit
- Ownership document / rent agreement
- NOC from Municipality (Trade License copy)
- Water test report (for manufacturers)
- Form B (application form)

## Fire NOC

**Q: What is a Fire NOC?**
A No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the Kerala Fire & Rescue Services is mandatory for establishments that pose a fire risk or have large public footfall, such as restaurants, hotels, malls, educational institutions, hospitals, and cinema halls.

**Q: Who needs Fire NOC in Kerala?**
- Restaurants and hotels
- Commercial establishments above 500 sqft
- Buildings with more than 15 metres height
- Public assembly buildings (cinemas, auditoriums)
- Industries storing flammable materials
- Hospitals and nursing homes

**Q: How to apply for Fire NOC in Kerala?**
1. Visit the Kerala Fire & Rescue Services website or nearest Fire Station
2. Submit application along with building plan / layout
3. Pay prescribed fee (varies by floor area)
4. Physical inspection by Fire Officer
5. Compliance of fire safety requirements (fire extinguishers, exit signs, emergency lighting, smoke detectors)
6. Issue of NOC after compliance

**Q: What is the validity of Fire NOC?**
Fire NOC is typically valid for 1–3 years and must be renewed before expiry.

## Factory License

**Q: Who needs a Factory License in Kerala?**
Under the Factories Act, 1948, any premises where:
- 10 or more workers are employed with the aid of power, OR
- 20 or more workers are employed without the aid of power
is considered a "Factory" and requires a Factory License.

**Q: How to apply for Factory License in Kerala?**
1. Submit application to the Directorate of Factories and Boilers, Kerala
2. Obtain approval from the Chief Inspector of Factories
3. Ensure compliance with safety, health, and welfare provisions of the Factories Act
4. Obtain Pollution Control Board (PCB) Consent to Establish (CTE) before construction
5. Obtain PCB Consent to Operate (CTO) before starting production

## Pollution Control Board (PCB) Consent

**Q: When is PCB consent required?**
Any industry that generates liquid effluents, gaseous emissions, or solid waste requires:
- **Consent to Establish (CTE)**: Before starting construction / installation
- **Consent to Operate (CTO)**: Before starting production/operations

**Q: What categories does PCB use?**
Kerala PCB categorises industries under:
- **Red Category**: Highly polluting (cement, chemicals, distilleries, large tanneries)
- **Orange Category**: Moderately polluting (food processing, pharmaceuticals, small textiles)
- **Green Category**: Low polluting (small bakeries, light engineering, handloom)
- **White Category**: Non-polluting (IT, paper trading, tailoring)

White category industries generally do not need PCB consent.

**Q: How to apply for PCB consent in Kerala?**
Apply online at: https://www.keralapcb.nic.in
Submit application with:
- Project report / EIA report (for Red category)
- Layout plan
- Industry details (raw materials, products, machinery)
- Water consumption and wastewater details

## MSME / Udyam Registration

**Q: What is Udyam Registration?**
Udyam Registration (formerly Udyog Aadhar) is the official MSME registration issued by the Ministry of MSME, Government of India. It provides businesses legal status as MSME, entitling them to government schemes, priority sector lending, subsidies, and other benefits.

**Q: What are the MSME size categories?**
- **Micro**: Investment ≤ ₹1 crore AND Turnover ≤ ₹5 crore
- **Small**: Investment ≤ ₹10 crore AND Turnover ≤ ₹50 crore
- **Medium**: Investment ≤ ₹50 crore AND Turnover ≤ ₹250 crore

**Q: How to register under Udyam?**
1. Visit: https://udyamregistration.gov.in
2. Enter Aadhaar number of the proprietor / director / partner
3. OTP verification
4. Fill in business details (name, type, NIC code, address, bank details)
5. Self-declare investment and turnover
6. Submit — Udyam Registration Certificate issued instantly (free of cost, no fee)

**Q: Is Udyam Registration free?**
Yes. Udyam Registration is completely free. There are no government fees. Beware of third-party websites charging fees for registration.
